How to Use This Tool

What Is This Tool?

This is a free assistant created by Thrive Advocacy Group to help parents and caregivers during IEP (Individualized Education Program) and ARD (Admission, Review, and Dismissal) meetings.

It can:

  • Answer your questions about special education law (IDEA, Section 504, and Texas law) in plain English
  • Transcribe your meeting in real-time so you have a record of what was said
  • Read your child's documents (IEPs, evaluations) to give you personalized answers — without ever uploading them

Getting Started (First Time Only)

The AI that answers your questions is powered by a free service called Groq. You'll need to create a free account and get an API key. Here's how:

  1. Go to console.groq.com/keys
  2. Click "Sign Up" — use your email or Google account (it's free, no credit card needed)
  3. Once logged in, click "Create API Key"
  4. Give it any name (like "IEP Tool") and click "Submit"
  5. Copy the key that appears (it starts with "gsk_...")
  6. Come back here and paste it into the setup screen

This only takes about 60 seconds and you only need to do it once. Your key is saved in your browser and never shared with anyone.

Using the Chat

Type any question about your child's special education rights. The assistant will answer in plain English and cite the specific laws so you can reference them in your meeting. Examples:

  • "What is FAPE and what does my child have a right to?"
  • "The school wants to reduce my child's speech therapy. Can they do that?"
  • "What questions should I ask about my child's IEP goals?"
  • "The school says they don't have to pay for an independent evaluation. Is that true?"
  • "Can I record the ARD meeting?"
  • "The school wants to suspend my child. What are my rights?"

Press Enter to send, or Shift+Enter for a new line.

Using Live Transcription

Click "Start Meeting" to begin recording and transcribing your meeting in real-time.

  • For in-person meetings: Place your phone or laptop near the speakers so the microphone can pick up the conversation
  • For virtual meetings (Zoom/Teams): The tool will transcribe audio from your computer's microphone
  • Your browser will ask for microphone permission — click "Allow"
  • Works best in Chrome or Microsoft Edge
  • Click "Copy" or "Download" to save your transcript after the meeting

Tip: On a phone, switch between the Chat and Transcript tabs at the top of the screen.

Loading Your Child's Documents

Click the "Documents" button to load your child's IEP, evaluations, or other records. This lets the assistant give you answers specific to your child.

  • Supports PDF, Word (.docx), and text (.txt) files
  • Your documents are processed entirely in your browser — they are never uploaded to any server
  • You can load multiple documents at once
  • Documents stay loaded until you close the browser tab

Your Privacy

  • Your documents never leave your device. They are read and processed entirely in your browser.
  • Your questions are sent to Groq's AI service (using your personal API key) to generate answers. Groq does not use your inputs for training.
  • No data is collected by Thrive Advocacy Group through this tool.
  • No account is needed. Your settings are saved in your browser's local storage.
  • To clear all saved data, go to Settings and clear your browser's site data.

Important Disclaimer

This tool provides information about special education law — it does not provide legal advice. The information is for educational purposes to help you understand your rights.

For complex situations, please consider:

  • Hiring a special education advocate — visit Thrive Advocacy Group
  • Consulting a special education attorney
  • Contacting your state's Parent Training and Information Center (PTI)
  • Filing a complaint with the Texas Education Agency (TEA)

Troubleshooting

  • "Thinking..." stays forever: Check your internet connection. If it persists, your API key may have expired — get a new one from console.groq.com.
  • Transcription not working: Make sure you're using Chrome or Edge. Click the lock icon in the address bar and make sure Microphone is set to "Allow."
  • "Rate limited" message: You've asked many questions quickly. Wait 60 seconds and try again. (Free tier allows 30 questions per minute.)
  • Documents won't load: Make sure the file is a PDF, .docx, or .txt file. Scanned PDFs (images of documents) may not work — try the Word version if you have one.
